FAQ

Questions

Do I need Nexus '28?

Yes. aARgon3 orders unlock models inside Nexus '28, which is free for Mac and Windows. Flash is always free there; your order unlocks the rest.

What's the difference between a plan and a tier?

The plan is how many people can use one order: Personal is you (two people on Pro and Max), Business is a team of 5 to 7, Enterprise is 100 or more. The tier is what everyone on the order gets: Standard, Plus, Pro or Max.

What do the LentOS and connection choices do?

They set up your Apoji system in Nexus '28. LentOS picks your theme and orb skin (Sonoma or Horizon). The connection picks how your Mac and phone talk: through Apoji Cloud, straight to your Mac first (HLS), or never through the cloud (Private Secure Gateway). Neither one changes the price, and you can change them later in Nexus.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. Your order keeps working until the end of the period you paid for. Use the link on your Stripe receipt, or email us.

Refunds?

If something's wrong, email us within 14 days of a payment and we'll make it right, including a refund. See the Terms of Sale.

Can I get something that isn't on the list?

Yes. Open the Custom tab under Plans, pick the people, models and chip you want, and send it. We email you a price with a secure Stripe link or invoice. Nothing is charged until you pay it.

Enterprise?

For 100 people or more we set up a quote and bill you by invoice. Email us with how many people and which tier.
